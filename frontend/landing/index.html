approvals first

Before arkos acts in Gmail, Calendar, GitHub or your other connected accounts, it shows you the exact action and waits for a yes, unless you've turned on autopilot.

a fresh sandbox

Each conversation gets its own machine with a shell and your folders. When the work is done, the machine goes away.

files that persist

Your files live in arkos's storage, not the sandbox, so they're there next time, in every project that uses them.
